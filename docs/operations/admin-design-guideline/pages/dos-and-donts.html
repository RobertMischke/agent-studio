Dos and Don'ts

Five compact comparisons turn the core rules into reviewable visual choices. The Don't variants retain the 11 August sighting material and its evidence classification.

Standard Dos and Don'ts Applied surfaces

Flat composition

Why: A flat shared plane makes hierarchy and comparison clearer without adding visual containers that carry no operational meaning.

Observed source: AGT-2606, real light-theme before and after captures, 11 August 2026.

Color semantics

Why: Semantic color stays legible across themes and accessibility modes when text, shape, and urgency carry the same meaning.

Observed source: the compact pipeline and viewer-header sightings recorded by AGT-2567 and AGT-2608.

Tooltips

Why: Canonical tooltips preserve scan density while remaining available on hover and keyboard focus with one shared behavior.

Observed source: AGT-2567, mocked before capture and rendered tooltip proof.

Chips

Why: A chip earns its space only when its compact boundary helps recognition, action, or comparison.

Observed source: AGT-2608, mocked light-theme before and after captures, 11 August 2026.

Density

Why: Operator density reduces scanning distance without shrinking controls below their interaction floor or compressing prose into a narrow column.

Observed source: AGT-2610, mocked light-theme before and after captures, 11 August 2026.
