Visual dos and don'ts

Six compact comparison pairs turn the core admin rules into reviewable visual checks. The first five Don't panels reuse the preserved violation captures cited by the 11 August review; the sixth preserves the later task-header pair.

Overview Applied surfaces
ADM-01

Flat composition

Why: Full-width structure keeps one operational surface legible; repeated rounded containers fragment it into competing cards.

ADM-09

Color semantics

Why: Semantic color preserves operator attention; a normal waiting state must not look like an incident.

ADM-04

Tooltips and disclosure

Why: A compact primary state preserves scan rhythm while accessible disclosure keeps exact diagnostics available.

ADM-05

Chips

Why: Chips are labels, not layout units; too many equally strong pills obscure hierarchy and repeat facts.

ADM-08

Density

Why: Operator density means compact chrome plus readable content, not squeezing prose until it becomes a word column.

ADM-01 · ADM-04

Task-header status zone

Why: Shared left and right edges preserve the header rhythm from the workspace banner through the status zone to the tab row. The AGT-2559 waiting-reason priority remains semantic truth; only its presentation changes.
