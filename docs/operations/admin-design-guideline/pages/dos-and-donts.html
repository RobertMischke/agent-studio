Dos and Don'ts

Five compact comparisons turn the core admin rules into reviewable geometry. Each Don't is traced from a preserved operator capture from the 11 August review line.

Overview Dos and Don'ts Applied surfaces

Flat composition

ADM-01 · ADM-02

Use alignment, spacing, and complete-surface tint for hierarchy; repeated frames turn one workspace into a collection of unrelated boxes.

Don't source: AGT-2613 / before-wide-light.png and before-wide-dark.png.

Color semantics

ADM-03 · ADM-09

Reserve green, amber, and red for their semantic states and keep row geometry invariant, so color never invents a second hierarchy.

Don't source: AGT-2565 / AGT-2565--activity-entry-grid-before-light--mocked.png.

Tooltips and disclosure

ADM-04

Keep the current state in the row and move explanation into one accessible tooltip or disclosure instead of expanding persistent chrome.

Don't source: AGT-2567 / pipeline-decision-human-review-before-light--mocked.png.

Chips

ADM-05 · ADM-07

Use chips for compact identity or a state that changes a decision, not as a decorative wrapper for every available field.

Don't source: AGT-2608 / workbench-viewer-header-before-light--mocked.png.

Operator density

ADM-08

Compact rows should increase usable work area; they must not squeeze editable content into fixed columns or reduce contrast.

Don't source: AGT-2610 / workbench-details-panel-before-light--mocked.png.
