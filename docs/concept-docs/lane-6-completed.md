# Completed

The `6-completed` lane holds tasks that are done and accepted. The work ran, the outcome was reviewed, and someone — or an automated gate — signed off. A card here represents a closed loop: its evidence (activity log, diff, reports) is preserved, but no further runs are expected.

## What to do here

Mostly nothing — and that is the point. Completed cards are a record you can revisit when you need the history of how something was built or decided. When the lane gets crowded, move finished work to `7-archive`, either one card at a time or with the lane's archive-all action, to keep the active board focused on what is still in flight. Reopening is rare: if a "completed" task turns out to need more work, raise a fresh task rather than reanimating this one.
