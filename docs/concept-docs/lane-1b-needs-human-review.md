# Needs Human Review

The `1b-needs-human-review` lane is where a task pauses because preparation surfaced a decision only a person should make. The automated intake step will not guess past a real fork in the road — an ambiguous requirement, a scope trade-off, a risky direction — so it parks the card here and waits.

## What to do here

Read the question, then decide. The card carries the context the intake step assembled and the specific point it is blocked on. Once you answer — by editing the prompt, choosing a direction, or adding the missing constraint — send the card on to `2-ready` so it can be picked up, or back to `1-preparation` if it needs more shaping. Cards do not leave this lane on their own; your decision is the thing that unblocks them.
