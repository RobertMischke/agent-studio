# Archive

The `7-archive` lane is the long-term resting place for work that is finished and no longer needs to be on the active board. Archiving does not delete anything: the job folder, its logs, diffs, and reports stay on disk under the project's `7-archive/` directory. Archiving simply gets old cards out of your daily view.

## What to do here

Treat it as cold storage. Cards arrive here from `6-completed`, individually or via the archive-all action, and the board shows only a bounded window of the most recent ones with a count of the rest. You rarely act on an archived card directly; when you need the full history, open the folder on disk. Keeping completed work flowing into the archive is what keeps the upstream lanes readable — the active board should reflect what is happening now, not everything that ever happened.
