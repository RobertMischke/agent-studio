# Backlog

The `0-backlog` lane is the holding area for raw, unrefined task ideas. Cards land here when you capture intent quickly and have not yet decided whether — or how — the work should run. Nothing is picked up from here automatically: the orchestrator never reaches into the backlog, so a card can sit safely until you are ready to shape it.

## What to do here

Triage. Pull a card into `1-preparation` when you want it refined into a runnable task, or delete it when the idea is stale. A backlog card is a note to yourself, not a commitment — keep the lane honest by clearing out what you will not act on, so the lanes downstream only ever hold real work.
