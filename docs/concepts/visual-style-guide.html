§3 · vNext

Decision space before delivery

The first modernization candidate is the current Welcome panel. It remains a candidate until the problem, options, decision and acceptance boundary are recorded.

Candidate 001 · Welcome panel

From generic launch card to useful return point

Observed

A centered card repeats product identity, lists projects, and offers New task / Open project chat. It is functional but visually generic and does not help a returning operator resume recent work.

Decision to make

Choose whether vNext prioritizes recency, operator intent, or a calmer brand moment. Do not combine all three without testing hierarchy and density.
