Choose by available context

Dots work when a nearby title and tooltip explain the state. Pips work only when their count or capacity contract is stable. Labels are the safest choice when ambiguity costs more than space.

Accessibility contract

Every indicator needs an accessible label and a text tooltip. Shape or glyph supplements color. A fixed trailing slot prevents row jitter, and reduced motion stops the running pulse.
